# ADR-018 — AWS End User Messaging Push and SMS are the initial MVP delivery providers, not notification-domain authority.

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The MVP needs mobile Push and critical SMS while retaining LimnoPulse-owned destinations, policy, delivery identity, budgets, and incident semantics. Platform and country readiness differ and must not leak into the canonical domain.

## Decision

Use AWS End User Messaging as the initial Push and SMS implementation behind the provider-neutral delivery port. Canonical platforms are `android` and `ios`; provider channel names and credentials remain adapter details.

## Consequences

One durable model supports platform rollout and provider replacement while isolated lanes bound failures. Commercial launch must wait for explicit platform, country, origination, privacy, and deliverability evidence.

## V4 traceability

V4 §§4, 17, 24 Phases 7B/7C, 27, and 31 require Android/FCM first, iOS/APNs second, and both before broad Brazil+United States launch. Production SMS is BR/US-only: Brazil requires shared/international-route and carrier readiness with no initial short code; the US requires registered toll-free readiness. Direct FCM/APNs adapters, Web Push, and alternative providers remain deferred.

## Implementation gate

Phase 7B independently proves Android then iOS registration, rotation, invalidation, environment separation, secure previews, and dual-platform launch readiness. The public destination contract must accept only canonical `android` and `ios` and reject provider channel names `GCM` and `APNS`; adapter fixtures must prove `android` maps to AWS `GCM` and `ios` maps to `APNS` or `APNS_SANDBOX`. Every Push registration or refresh must authenticate the current principal and verify current ACTIVE tenant membership; a missing, invalid, or mismatched principal must be rejected even when the token is unclaimed, and inactive membership must be rejected independently. On both Android and iOS, registration must reject cross-user and cross-tenant claims for a token already owned by another user or tenant and must not overwrite the existing owner. Before Phase 7B launch, every raw Push token must be encrypted and never returned after write. FCM service-account JSON and APNs private-key payloads must never enter OpenTofu state; when infrastructure provisioning cannot avoid state exposure, credentials must use secure post-provisioning or secret deployment. Each Push destination must be keyed by tenant, recipient, client app, and client app instance; multiple devices for one user must coexist and fan out independently, and registration or rotation of one instance must not overwrite another. A provider per-address permanent Push failure must conditionally invalidate only the destination version observed by the Attempt; a late failure for version N must preserve rotated version N+1. A provider-returned updated FCM/GCM token must be conditionally persisted as a fenced rotation against the destination version observed by the Attempt; it must not be ignored, and a late provider response must not overwrite a newer client rotation. A definite throttled or temporary Push per-address result, including 429 or 5xx, must retry with bounds rather than being dropped or retried without limit; ambiguous and permanent outcomes retain their separate rules. Android/FCM and iOS/APNs must have separate kill switches; each switch must stop only its own platform while preserving durable state, the other platform, and every other channel. An independent Push kill switch must stop Push while preserving durable state and leaving email, Telegram, and SMS intact. Successful provider Push acceptance must never become user incident acknowledgement and must leave incident acknowledgement and escalation state unchanged. A Push transport timeout after potential provider acceptance must become ambiguous or unknown and must not be automatically retried or resent. Phase 7C applies the common SMS gates to both Brazil and the United States: verified and consented destinations, critical-only acknowledgement-aware escalation, exact `PlanVersion` count and USD budget/max-price enforcement, single-part GSM-7/UCS-2 preflight with multipart rejection, durable reservation and provider-call semantics, idempotent delayed/duplicate/out-of-order delivery-receipt reconciliation, spend/price/storm controls, and an independent SMS kill switch. The Phase 7C pre-dispatch transaction must require present and fresh country configuration, route validation, origination reference, current price, and registration state; any absent or stale readiness evidence must fail closed before the provider call. Before production, every readiness evidence category must define its authoritative source and an objective expiration rule using `expires_at` or maximum age; subjective freshness or evidence with no expiry must fail closed. The provider `ProtectConfiguration` country policy itself must allow only BR and US and block every other country; the application country allowlist is independent and must not substitute for this provider control. Distinct logical SMS jobs racing for the same tenant's last count slot or USD budget must contend atomically on one conditional budget-period version without Redis; at most one winner may reserve and contact the provider, and every loser must defer or fail before the call so the tenant cannot overspend. In the pre-dispatch transaction, a current-country price above the PlanVersion cap must prevent `SendTextMessage` and consume no call count; provider `MaxPrice` remains defense in depth and must not replace this guard. The final pre-provider transaction must recheck active recipient membership and authorization; a recipient revoked after queueing must not receive the SMS. Phase 7C must use the exact approved critical SMS templates: `pt-BR` `LimnoPulse: incidente crítico. Abra o app e confirme.` and `en-US` `LimnoPulse: critical incident. Open the app and acknowledge.`; content must exclude tenant, site/asset, location, precise telemetry, personal/phone, command, actuator, credential, token, and other sensitive fields. Every validation or other failure before `SendTextMessage` starts must release both the reserved count slot and the reserved USD amount. Phase 7C request fixtures must prove `SendTextMessage` uses `MessageType=TRANSACTIONAL`, the approved origination reference, protection configuration, SMS configuration set, bounded TTL, and provider `MaxPrice`. Phase 7C BR and US tests must prove SMS origination references, pools, and routes are environment-bound and cannot cross development, staging, or production. Every verified E.164 SMS destination must store its number using application-level envelope encryption at rest throughout its lifecycle; plaintext phone numbers must remain excluded from ordinary reads, queue jobs, logs, metrics, and ordinary audit records. Each SMS verification challenge must use a separate Attempt with digest, TTL, attempt and rate limits, its own anti-abuse controls, and a separate platform budget; Phase 7C tests must prove it cannot share or bypass the critical-escalation Attempt or budget. Duplicate standard SQS Push or SMS jobs, including concurrent multiprocess delivery, must be idempotent per scheduled attempt: the same logical job and retry-attempt identity yields one durable Attempt, one provider attempt, and one cost commitment; a bounded retry of a definite temporary failure creates a new explicit retry Attempt identity while retaining one Delivery, and duplicate redelivery of that retry attempt must not call the provider twice. A `SendTextMessage` timeout after potential provider acceptance must leave the Attempt unknown or awaiting provider feedback; it must not be automatically retried or resent, preventing duplicate messages and duplicate cost. Across Push and SMS, raw token, phone number, and message body values must be absent from queue jobs, logs, metrics, and ordinary audit records. Country readiness remains separate: every non-BR/US country is blocked, Brazil remains blocked until shared/international-route validation and carrier tests pass, and the United States remains blocked until registered toll-free approval plus STOP/HELP and opt-in/privacy/terms controls pass. Alongside route and carrier readiness, the Brazil gate must prove in-app destination management and opt-out, disclose that the displayed origin is unstable, and make no promise of replies or a fixed originating number.

Every SMS destination create, verify, and delete request must authenticate the current principal and verify current ACTIVE tenant membership; missing, invalid, mismatched, inactive, cross-user, or cross-tenant requests must be rejected, even when the destination is pending or unclaimed. Phase 7B must version, rotate, and roll back FCM service-account and APNs credentials; invalid or expired replacements must fail closed without leaving a compromised credential active, and the credential rotation/rollback runbooks and tests must be proven before launch. Phase 7C launch readiness must enable and exercise AWS account/enforced provider spend limits and billing alarms as an outer boundary in addition to tenant budgets, per-message caps, and storm controls.

Every Push destination revoke and delete request must authenticate the current principal and verify current ACTIVE tenant membership; missing, invalid, mismatched, inactive, cross-user, or cross-tenant requests must be rejected. Phase 7B adapter fixtures must prove that an overall provider `200` containing a per-address permanent failure is parsed as a permanent failure for that address and conditionally invalidates only the observed destination version.

Before Phase 7B launch, every raw Push token must use application-level envelope encryption through KMS or the Database Encryption SDK and must never be returned after write; server-side table encryption alone is insufficient. Phase 7B must fail closed when an iOS destination, token, credential, or channel reference is tagged for the wrong APNs sandbox/production environment; APNS and APNS_SANDBOX references are non-interchangeable and mismatch tests are required. Every Push and SMS destination lifecycle mutation—create, verify, registration, refresh, revoke, delete, invalidation, and opt-out—must use optimistic versioning/conditional writes; stale races must not resurrect or overwrite newer state. Before any SMS verification challenge sends, Phase 7C must verify tenant SMS eligibility and entitlement, including Trial/Starter/suspended denial; only an explicitly audited administrative import of already-verified destinations may bypass that check. Phase 7C message preflight must count GSM-7 extension escapes and concatenation thresholds exactly, enforce one-part GSM-7/UCS-2 limits, reject multipart messages, and regression-test template changes. After possible Push provider acceptance becomes ambiguous or unknown, the orchestrator must not automatically fall back to SMS or another channel unless a versioned policy explicitly accepts duplicate risk. Phase 7B/7C launch readiness must provision separate Push and SMS dispatch queues and DLQs with bounded poison-message redrive, queue-age/DLQ alarms, and recovery tests; feedback-consumer DLQs do not substitute.

Every Push and SMS destination lifecycle mutation—create, verify, rotate, invalidate, revoke, delete, and opt-out—and every cross-user token-claim rejection must emit an immutable audit event. After account lifecycle deletion, raw Push tokens and phone numbers must be erased; only non-reversible hashes and delivery evidence may remain when policy/legal retention permits, and erasure tests must prove tombstoned destinations retain no decryptable PII.

Phase 7C US readiness must require the toll-free origination number/pool registration use case to be exactly Notifications; any use-case mismatch must fail closed before dispatch.

Phase 7C current SMS charge guard must include both provider country price and carrier fees when comparing total per-message cost to PlanVersion maximum; a carrier surcharge above the cap must fail closed before provider dispatch.

Phase 7C must enforce durable SMS storm/rate windows independently per recipient, tenant, and event family before provider dispatch; recipient-scoped limits must prevent one recipient from receiving every family allowance.

Before creating a retry Attempt for a definite temporary failure, the worker must derive a deterministic successor identity from the Delivery ID, failed Attempt ID, and the failed Attempt's persisted retry ordinal plus one; a conditional write must atomically bind that ordinal to the Delivery and failed Attempt and create at most one successor Attempt, so concurrent workers converge on one identity and only that Attempt may contact the provider.

## Non-goals

This record does not make AWS EUM canonical, equate acceptance with receipt or acknowledgement, provision a Brazilian short code, add 10DLC, support marketing traffic, or implement direct FCM/APNs or Web Push now.
